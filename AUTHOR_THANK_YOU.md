# Thank you to the paper authors

Thank you to Dennis Frauen, Athiya Deviyani, Mihaela van der Schaar, and
Stefan Feuerriegel for developing DMLRank and for making the paper, claim
structure, and implementation trail available for independent study.

The work provides a valuable case study for evaluating statistical claims in
LLM leaderboards, especially the interaction between selective preference
data, efficient influence functions, and acquisition policies.

This note is an expression of appreciation only. It does not imply that the
authors reviewed, endorsed, or maintain this repository.
