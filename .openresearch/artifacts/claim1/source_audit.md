# Claim 1 source audit

Section 4 defines a Generalized Average Ranking Score by
`theta = E[F(mu(X))]`, where `mu_jk,c(x)` is the conditional probability of
outcome category `c` for ordered pair `(j,k)` and `F` is a known
differentiable map. Section 4.1 equation (3), Section 4.2 equations (4)--(6),
and Appendix C instantiate the Bradley--Terry projection, weighted Borda, and
Rank Centrality maps.

The statement is definitional plus algebraic: the special cases must be
derived from the same ordered-pair probability tensor, not approximated by
unrelated ranking algorithms. The Bradley--Terry recovery check assumes a
valid logistic latent-strength model. Rank Centrality assumes a well-defined
stochastic transition matrix and unique stationary solution.
