# Claim 2 source audit

Theorem 5.1, equations (9)--(13), and Appendix B state a conditional theorem.
Assumptions include i.i.d. sampling, missing at random, positivity, a
differentiable locally Lipschitz `F`, finite EIF second moment, at least
two-fold cross-fitting, a lower-bounded fitted propensity, and the stated
product-rate conditions for the outcome and propensity nuisances.

The quantified conclusion is asymptotic: the one-step estimator has an
asymptotically linear expansion, converges at `sqrt(n)` to the displayed
Gaussian law, and attains the semiparametric efficiency bound. A finite
normality simulation alone cannot verify that conclusion.
